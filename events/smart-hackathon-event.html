NFSU SMART Hackathon

27 February 2026 · NFSU Tripura Campus
Build · Present · Compete

Hackathon Overview

NFSU SMART Hackathon brought developers together to solve real-world problems through rapid ideation, engineering and teamwork. Student teams collaborated to transform concepts into usable prototypes within a competitive environment.

Event Highlights

  • Team-based problem solving
  • Rapid application prototyping
  • Technical presentation
  • Peer and mentor evaluation
  • Real-world project experience

Event Details

Participants worked across the full project cycle: defining the problem, dividing technical responsibilities, building a focused implementation and presenting results. The event encouraged practical innovation and exposed students to the pace and collaboration of competitive development.
