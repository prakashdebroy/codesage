Fund My Crazy

4 September 2026 · NFSU Tripura Campus
Innovation & Entrepreneurship

Event Overview

Fund My Crazy brought together students with ambitious ideas and encouraged them to present innovation as a convincing, structured pitch. The event was organized by Code Sage with Google Student Ambassadors Priyanshu Sahoo and Prasunma Das.

70Participants
3Core Teams
1Innovation Goal

What Students Learned

  • Problem discovery
  • Idea validation
  • Pitch storytelling
  • Market and feasibility thinking
  • Confident presentation

Event Details

Participants explored unconventional ideas, identified target users and communicated how their concepts could create measurable value. Feedback from peers and organizers helped teams refine both the product story and practical next steps.
